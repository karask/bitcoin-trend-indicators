# Gold and silver futures

Yahoo Finance `GC=F` / `SI=F`, COMEX gold/silver futures in USD per troy ounce. These are neither spot metals nor ETFs. Quote metadata identifies the currently quoted contract; the historical series follows Yahoo's contract selection. Roll/back-adjustment is not independently verified.

Initial audit on 9 September 2026: 1,933 validated completed daily candles per metal from 2 January 2019 through 8 September 2026. Earlier data contained close-outside-high/low candles, absent 2016 sessions and an all-null 29 January 2018 bar. The 2019 cutoff excludes this unreliable older segment; prices are not fabricated or clamped. Zero-volume bars are retained and disclosed as a potential thin-market limitation.

This is a conservative EOD publication model, **not a COMEX exchange-hours calendar**. A bar becomes complete at the next 00:00 UTC after its provider date. US cash-session dates provide a minimum publication baseline; valid additional weekday holiday bars are retained, and all-null holiday placeholders are ignored. Missing baseline dates, duplicate dates and invalid OHLC fail validation. Monday-based weeks become available at Saturday 00:00 UTC and require all baseline dates. No exchange settlement time or exact next-open timestamp is inferred. A holiday countdown may reach a cutoff without a new bar.

History is seeded once into the existing D1 tables under separate commodity IDs. Opening the page checks for newer completed data; a stale snapshot fetches a 400-calendar-day overlap from Yahoo. Changed overlapping values trigger a full source rebase. Candle writes and the new snapshot are one atomic D1 batch. Requests share the existing 60-second per-market refresh reservation. No commodity cron is added. IndexedDB holds full private browser histories and merges D1 tails; corrections trigger a full D1 read. APIs require the existing session and are no-store. Account deletion also clears commodity browser history. No provider key is needed.

All applicable existing indicators run unchanged except for the requested weekly KK screenshot fits: Gold 10/2 and Silver 15/2.4 (see `kk-2026-09-09/REPORT.md`). Daily KK and unidentified commodity contexts retain the uncalibrated 10/3 baseline. No crypto/stock preset leaks into commodities. Research uses next-available-bar-open execution, 5/15/30-bps sensitivity, the same price-series benchmark and four-year rolling tests, and 252/52 daily/weekly annualization. These are **unlevered continuous-price simulations**, not executable futures returns: contract roll costs, margin, leverage and financing are excluded.

Commands: `node --experimental-strip-types scripts/seed-commodities-d1.ts --symbol=GC=F` (prepare only), add `--apply --remote` to seed that metal. Symbols are processed sequentially; scope refreshes to the selected metal. No new D1 schema migration or refresh-worker deployment is required.
